# Decision memo: offer a different time for the second dietary recall?

**To:** Pilot sponsor · **From:** Evaluation lead · **Decision requested:** Approve a small scheduling test at Sites A and B, conditional on verifying the shortfall.

This is a fictional tabletop exercise for the [six-site nutrition pilot](charter.md), not a report from an actual program. All counts and decision thresholds below are illustrative.

### What prompted the decision

| Site | Valid second recalls / people due | Completion |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 12 / 24 | 50% |
| B | 15 / 25 | 60% |
| Other four sites combined | 66 / 80 | 82.5% |

Each person in the denominator completed a first recall and had a second-recall window that closed this month. The numerator counts a valid second recall within that window. A rescheduled appointment is not a second person. These counts do not tell us why a recall is absent, whether participants are disengaged, or whether the nutrition program works.

### Recommendation and ownership

1. **Reconcile before contacting anyone.** Within two working days, the data steward and site coordinators will match appointment records to uploads, remove duplicate bookings, and check due dates and delayed or rejected forms. Recalculate both counts. If late uploads explain the gap, fix the transfer and do not add calls.
2. **If the gap persists, test access to appointments.** The nutrition-assessment lead will ask both site coordinators about available hours, language coverage, and cancellation reasons. If hours are a barrier, offer one evening block per week for two weeks, using existing staff time and participants’ approved contact preferences. Keep the recall window, instrument, and contact limit unchanged. The sponsor can approve this test; extra spending or a protocol change needs separate review.
3. **Return with a decision next month.** The evaluation lead will report the same numerator and denominator by site for the next closed-window cohort, alongside evening-slot use, staff hours, and opt-outs. Continue for one more cycle only if completion rises by at least 10 percentage points at **each** site, within the agreed staffing and contact limits. If counts are too small to interpret, report them and recommend whether to extend observation; if burden rises or scheduling is not the barrier, stop and investigate with the sites.

This is a feasibility decision, not an estimate of the effect of evening appointments. Record the sponsor's choice and owner in the decision log; carry unresolved measurement risk forward under [R001](risk-register.csv).
