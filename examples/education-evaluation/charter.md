# Literacy-support pilot evaluation charter

## Project identity

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Project title | Literacy-support pilot outcome evaluation |
| Sponsor | Director of Programs |
| Project lead | Research Operations Manager |
| Evaluation lead | Quantitative Research Scientist |
| Data owner | Data Services Lead |
| Planned duration | 16 weeks |
| Sites | 12 fictional implementation sites |

## Purpose and decision context

The organization piloted an intensive literacy-support model and must decide whether to continue, revise, or expand it for the next academic year. The evaluation will estimate short-term outcome differences, document implementation, and identify conditions that should inform the decision.

## Objectives

| Objective | Success measure | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Estimate the participant outcome difference | Approved effect estimate with uncertainty and sensitivity checks | Technical report | Evaluation lead |
| Assess whether the model was delivered as intended | Site-level implementation indicators reconciled and summarized | Implementation brief | Program lead |
| Deliver evidence before budget planning | Final decision brief accepted by end of week 15 | Approval record | Project lead |

## Scope

**In scope**

- eligible participants in the twelve pilot sites
- prespecified outcome and implementation measures
- comparison construction from approved administrative data
- technical report and executive decision brief

**Out of scope**

- long-term outcomes beyond the available observation window
- cost-effectiveness analysis
- causal claims not supported by the approved design
- public release of site-level findings

## Key deliverables

| Deliverable | Acceptance criteria | Approver | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evaluation plan | Questions, estimand, sources, models, diagnostics, and limitations approved | Sponsor | Week 3 |
| Data-readiness memo | Population counts reconciled and critical validation findings resolved | Data owner | Week 7 |
| Technical report | Reproducible estimates, diagnostics, sensitivity checks, and limitations | Evaluation lead | Week 13 |
| Decision brief | Findings translated accurately for the expansion decision | Sponsor | Week 15 |

## Governance

The evaluation lead owns methodological recommendations. The data owner approves extract definitions and access. The project lead owns delivery and escalation. The sponsor approves scope, material changes, and external use. Any change to the primary outcome, comparison definition, or delivery date requires a logged change request.
