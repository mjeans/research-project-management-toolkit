# Stage gates

Stage gates create explicit points where the sponsor and project lead decide whether the work is ready to advance. A gate can be approved, approved with conditions, held for remediation, or stopped.

## Gate 1: Initiation approved

Required evidence:

- charter sponsor and decision owner identified
- evaluation purpose and intended use documented
- scope boundaries and major assumptions agreed
- key stakeholders identified
- preliminary feasibility and ethical considerations reviewed

## Gate 2: Design ready

Required evidence:

- evaluation questions mapped to measures and methods
- population, comparison strategy, and analytic unit defined
- data sources and access pathway confirmed
- power or precision considerations documented when applicable
- limitations and decision thresholds discussed with stakeholders
- protocol, privacy, security, and IRB determinations documented

## Gate 3: Data ready

Required evidence:

- approved data specification and transfer process
- required agreements and access controls in place
- reproducible ingestion and validation checks completed
- missingness, duplication, linkage, and outlier findings reviewed
- analysis population reconciled to the approved definition
- deviations recorded and accepted

## Gate 4: Analysis ready for interpretation

Required evidence:

- prespecified primary analyses completed
- diagnostics and sensitivity checks reviewed
- code or syntax peer reviewed
- results independently reconciled to expected counts
- deviations from the analysis plan documented
- disclosure and small-cell rules applied

## Gate 5: Release approved

Required evidence:

- conclusions supported by the reported results
- limitations and uncertainty clearly communicated
- technical and nontechnical deliverables aligned
- stakeholder review comments resolved or logged
- accessibility, privacy, and publication requirements met
- final approver and release date recorded

## Gate 6: Closeout complete

Required evidence:

- deliverables accepted
- files, code, metadata, and decisions archived
- access removed or transferred as required
- lessons learned documented
- follow-up actions assigned
- benefits or use-of-findings review scheduled when appropriate
